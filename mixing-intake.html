Royalty & credits

Standard flat-fee mixing does not automatically create a royalty claim. If this project has a negotiated mixer point, producer participation, songwriting split, SoundExchange direction, or another royalty arrangement, the project will use a separate signed agreement.

Royalty delivery protection

If royalty participation is required for this project, final delivery stays locked until the agreement is executed and signed by both the client and MixedByRashid. Projects with no royalty agreement requirement are not blocked by this step.

Private by design

Files are not submitted by email and are not intended to live at public download URLs. Access will be tied to the verified paid order and the client project.
