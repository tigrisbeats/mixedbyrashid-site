Your private mastering workspace unlocks after Stripe confirms your paid mastering order.

Final mix upload

  • Upload the final stereo WAV directly on the site
  • 24-bit or 32-bit float preferred
  • Original sample rate
  • No clipping or normalization
  • Up to 5 GB per file
  • Up to 25 GB total per paid order

Reference tracks & direction

  • Add one or more reference tracks
  • Include artist name, song title, release platforms, and desired feel
  • Call out any concerns about the mix
  • Keep references private inside this project

A/B comparison

Compare the submitted mix, reference track, current master, and revision from the same project player.

Revision & delivery

One mastering revision is included. Revision notes, version history, approval, and the final downloadable master will remain attached to this project.

Royalty & credits

Standard flat-fee mastering does not automatically create a royalty claim. If a separate royalty, participation, songwriting, or SoundExchange arrangement applies, it must be documented separately for this project.

View Royalty & Credits Workflow

Royalty delivery protection

If royalty participation is required, final delivery stays locked until the agreement is executed and signed by both sides. Projects with no royalty agreement requirement move through normal mastering delivery without this extra gate.

Private by design

No email attachments or public cloud links. Access will be tied to the verified paid order and the client project.
