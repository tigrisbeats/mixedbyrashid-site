Mixing Submission

Already paid? Use this checklist so your mix can start without file ping-pong.

Send these files

  • Consolidated WAV stems starting at the same timestamp
  • Dry lead vocal stems when available
  • Wet/effected versions if an effect is part of the sound
  • Instrumental stems or full beat
  • BPM and song title
  • A rough mix, if you have one
  • 1–2 reference songs, optional but helpful

Include these notes

  • Artist name
  • Song title
  • What you want the record to feel like
  • Any effects or moments that must stay
  • Anything you definitely do not want changed

Turnaround & revisions

Typical turnaround is 3–5 days after payment and complete files are received. Two revisions are included within the original creative direction.

For large files, upload them to your preferred cloud storage and paste the share link into the email.
