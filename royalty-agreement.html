Royalty & Credits

This step is used only when a project includes an agreed royalty, producer/mixer point, songwriting split, SoundExchange direction, or another negotiated participation. Standard flat-fee mixing and mastering orders do not automatically create royalties.

Legal-review status

Non-production draft. The agreement template must be reviewed and approved by an entertainment attorney before client signatures can be activated. The portal will not treat this screen as a production contract until that approval is recorded.

Agreement setup

The project record stores the agreement version, status, royalty basis, notes, and attorney-review status.

Participants & splits

Each participant can store legal/display name, role, royalty percentage, composition share, master share, PRO affiliation, IPI/CAE, and SoundExchange payee information where applicable.

Declared full-song composition splits are validated to total 100% before execution.

Credits & release metadata

  • Required credit name and role
  • Artist and song title
  • ISRC and UPC when available
  • Release date, label, and distributor
  • Client confirmation of required credits

Signature & delivery gate

For royalty-participating projects, final delivery remains locked until the agreement is marked executed and both the client and MixedByRashid signatures are recorded.

Agreement events are logged for creation, updates, sending, signatures, execution, and voiding.

Project status flow

Draft → Pending Client → Pending MixedByRashid → Executed → Final Delivery Unlocked

If the agreement is voided or incomplete, final delivery stays locked for that royalty-participating project.
