Service Policies

Clear expectations so we can spend less time sorting out logistics and more time making records.

Studio Time

Studio time is $60 per hour with a 2-hour minimum, making the minimum session total $120.

A $60 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your session. Your booking is not confirmed until the deposit has been paid.

The remaining $60 minimum-session balance is due at the time of your session.

Rescheduling

If you need to change your session, contact MixedByRashid as early as possible.

Rescheduling is based on availability. Studio deposits are non-refundable and are not automatically refundable if you are unable to attend your scheduled session.

Mixing

Mixing is $50 per song and is paid upfront.

Typical turnaround is 3–5 days after payment and all required files are received.

Two revisions are included within the original creative direction.

Mastering

Mastering is $20 per song and is paid upfront.

Typical turnaround is 1–3 days after payment and the final mix is received.

One revision is included within the original mastering direction.

Revisions & Major Changes

Included revisions cover reasonable adjustments within the original direction of the project.

Major creative changes, replacement recordings, new stems, or a new direction after work has begun may require an additional fee.

File Delivery

Clients are responsible for providing complete, organized, and accessible files.

Turnaround time begins once payment and all required project files have been received.

QUESTIONS?

If you need clarification before booking or ordering a service, contact MixedByRashid.
