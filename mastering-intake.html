Mastering Submission

Already paid? Send your final mix using the checklist below so your master is ready to move.

Send your final mix

  • Final stereo WAV file
  • 24-bit or 32-bit float preferred
  • Export at the original sample rate
  • Make sure the mix is not clipping
  • Leave the master limiter off when possible
  • Do not normalize the export

Include these details

  • Artist name
  • Song title
  • Release platforms
  • Desired overall feel
  • Any concerns you have about the mix
  • Reference track, optional
Start Submission Email

Turnaround & revisions

Typical mastering turnaround is 1–3 days after payment and the final mix is received. One revision is included within the original mastering direction.

If your WAV file is too large for email, upload it to your preferred cloud storage and paste the share link into the submission email.
