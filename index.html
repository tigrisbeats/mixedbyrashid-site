Studio booking policy

The studio deposit is 50% of the 2-hour minimum and is non-refundable. A session is not secured until the deposit has been paid. The remaining minimum-session balance is due at the session.

Need to change a session? Contact MixedByRashid as early as possible. Rescheduling is handled case by case based on availability.

Remote service policy

Mixing and mastering are paid upfront. Turnaround begins after payment and all required files are received. Revision limits apply to changes within the original creative direction.

FAQ

The questions artists usually ask before the first session or upload.

How does the studio deposit work?

Studio time is $60/hour with a 2-hour minimum. You pay a $60 non-refundable deposit to secure the booking. The remaining $60 minimum balance is due at the session.

When do I choose my studio time?

After a successful studio deposit payment, you are directed to the scheduling calendar to choose an available 2-hour session.

How do I send files for mixing?

Export and organize your stems, include BPM and any creative notes, then follow the mixing submission instructions after payment.

What should I send for mastering?

Send your finished stereo mix at full resolution without clipping. Leave master-bus limiting off when possible unless it is an intentional part of your sound.

How long does mixing take?

Typical mixing turnaround is 3–5 days after payment and complete files are received.

How long does mastering take?

Typical mastering turnaround is 1–3 days after payment and the final mix is received.

How many revisions are included?

Mixing includes 2 revisions. Mastering includes 1 revision. New creative direction or replacement recordings may require a new service fee.
